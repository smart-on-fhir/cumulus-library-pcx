# Inputs

- `include_*.csv`: encounters from 2008-01-01 with history, ages 0-120, all genders, 2 or more
  encounters over 365 or more days, laboratory Observations. See PROTOCOL.md section 2.
- `age_group.csv`: Infant (0), Early childhood (1-4), Childhood (5-11), Adolescent (12-17),
  Young adult (18-25), Adult (26-64), Older adult (65-120).
- `casedef.csv`: `subtype,system,code,display,tier` with subtypes medulloblastoma, atrt,
  cns_embryonal, etmr, pineoblastoma and tiers 1-2.
- `dx_*`, `rx_chemo_*`, `rx_contrast_methotrexate`, `lab_*`, `proc_*`: brain-tumor diagnoses,
  chemotherapy agents, toxicity laboratories, craniotomy and radiation procedures.
- `client_dictionary.csv`, `data_dictionary.csv`: export dictionaries for the opt-in client views
  and the Cumulus manifest.

Codes are strings: preserve leading zeroes. Valuesets are discovered by filename
(`<aspect>_<name>.csv`, aspects dx, rx, lab, obs, proc, diag, doc, enc, allergy); required columns
are `system,code,display`. `file_upload_*.toml` are generated by `cumulus-study build`; never edit
them. Reviewed codes are recorded in PROTOCOL.md section 3. Use the valueset, rxnorm and loinc skills to find and verify codes.
